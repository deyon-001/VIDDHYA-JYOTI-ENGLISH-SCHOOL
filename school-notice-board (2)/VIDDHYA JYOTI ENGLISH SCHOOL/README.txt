Viddhya Jyoti English School - Notices and Results website

Pages: index.html (home), notices.html, results.html, admin.html (principal only)

First time: open admin.html and create the principal password.
The principal can publish notices and student results (paste a CSV marks sheet),
change the password, and download/restore a backup.

Data is saved in the browser (localStorage), so it appears only on the computer
where it was published. Use Settings > Download backup to move data.
